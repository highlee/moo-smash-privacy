Privacy notice

Last updated and effective 19 November 2026

This notice covers Math Test when you open it in an app that uses SUD OpenPaaS. The individual developer and operator is 游明珠. It explains how the game creates your learning report, remembers your choices, and lets you or your guardian manage those records.

What the game saves during practice

The game keeps practice records in the local storage that your host app provides for this game. You can pick a grade and start practising. The game does not ask for your name, school, phone number or government ID to practise.

The home screen remembers the last grade or custom mode you started. The game records practice dates, session and question counts, correct answers, results by topic and the number of mistakes you corrected. It uses these records to calculate accuracy, show learning reports and suggest topics to revisit. The game does not sync these learning records to a server run by the developer.

How long mistakes and reports are kept

The review list keeps up to 80 distinct missed questions, including the expression, correct answer and topic. When you answer one correctly during a review session and finish the session, the game removes it from the list.

Your report shows today or the last seven days. The game keeps entries for up to 14 practice dates so it can compare two weeks. Totals by topic are stored separately and remain until the corresponding local data is cleared.

Language and display

The game reads the language reported by your device or host app to choose Chinese or English. You can change languages on the home screen. The game saves your latest choice locally.

Ads and optional video rewards

The game integrates SUD OpenPaaS banner ads and optional rewarded videos. As of the date of this notice, ad placements have not been enabled.

When ads are enabled, the game uses the window size and safe display area supplied by the host to position banners. You can choose to watch a video to unlock custom practice for the day. The game uses the completion result reported by the ad service and saves the unlock date locally. Closing the video early leaves custom practice locked. Regular grade practice remains available.

The host app and third-party services

The app you open the game in uses SUD OpenPaaS to run it. How the host handles its own accounts and logs, and what an ad service processes when enabled, depends on the app and services you use. You can review that app’s privacy and third-party notices and the SUD Privacy Policy. The game does not send your review list or learning report to the ad service.

Viewing or clearing records and contacting us

Open mistake review or the learning report to view your records. You can change languages on the home screen. If the host app lets you clear data for an individual game, you can use that option to erase this game’s local records. This also removes your practice history, language choice and daily unlock. If you cannot find the option, email us with the name of the host app for help.

Players and guardians can email 371652098@qq.com to ask about access, copies, corrections, deletion or other privacy matters. Describe the issue and name the host app. You do not need to send a child’s name, school or government ID. The developer uses your email address and message to reply and keeps the correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Your learning records are on your device. The developer cannot retrieve or erase them remotely by email.

Children and guardians

If you are a child, you can read this notice with a parent or guardian and ask them before choosing to watch a video. A parent or guardian can use the email above to ask about a child’s practice records or the ads they see.

Visiting this page and future changes

GitHub Pages hosts this notice. When you visit the page, GitHub logs your IP address for security. This page has no added analytics scripts. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If the game changes how it handles information, we will update both language versions and the effective date here. You can use the contact email above to ask the developer about a change.
