Moo Smash Privacy Notice

For the SUD OpenPaaS edition
Last updated: 2 November 2026 · Effective: 2 November 2026

This notice is provided by 游明珠, the individual developer and operator of Moo Smash (Chinese title: 牛气碰碰), referred to as “we” or “us”. It explains how we handle information when providing the game and responding to enquiries. Contact: 371652098@qq.com.

This notice covers the game distributed through SUD OpenPaaS and played within a host app. It does not replace the separate privacy notices of the host app, platform or other editions of the game.

1. Information we process

We handle information only to the extent needed for specific functions. The current game version uses:

  • Local game data: unlocked and completed levels, challenge scores and play statistics, item and skin states, tutorial completion flags, sound preferences and other game settings. The game reads and writes this data in the host app’s local storage on your device to save progress, display results, restore settings and provide gameplay features.
  • Display information: the language setting, window or screen dimensions, device pixel ratio, safe area, status bar and menu position supplied by the host. The game uses this information to choose a language, fit the display and keep controls clear of system interfaces.
  • Interaction events: taps, drags and foreground/background events are processed while the game runs to respond to input, pause or resume play and activate power-saving mode. We have not added a separate behavioural analytics or screen-recording service to the game.
  • Enquiries you send us: if you contact us by email, we handle your email address, message and any screenshots or explanations you choose to provide. We use them to reply, investigate problems and handle privacy requests. Please do not send identity documents, passwords or other sensitive information unrelated to your enquiry.

The current OpenPaaS edition has no developer-operated account registration or cloud-save service. It does not actively request your nickname, avatar, gender, region, phone number, identity number or friends list through platform profile APIs, and does not offer a friends leaderboard or friend-overtake notifications. We do not request access to contacts, precise location, the camera or microphone through the game. Game voices are pre-recorded audio; they do not require recording your voice. Any sign-in, identity verification or permissions used by the host app are explained separately by that app.

2. Third-party plugins, SDKs and platform services

2.1 SUD OpenPaaS and the host app

The game depends on the SUD OpenPaaS runtime integrated into the host app for game loading, resource caching, local storage, display and lifecycle functions. SUD’s official privacy policy identifies its service providers as SUD Global Inc. and its affiliates. Please refer to the app you use for details of its own operator and the entities involved in that host’s services.

The information read directly by the game and its purposes are listed in section 1. The platform and host app may process account, device, network or operational log information for their own services. Their actual data categories, purposes, storage locations, permissions and rights procedures are described in their respective notices. See the official SUD Privacy Policy and the privacy notice available in your host app. Information independently processed by a platform is not automatically information collected by this game’s developer.

2.2 Advertising and other plugins

The version covered by this notice has no configured developer-side OpenPaaS advertising placements and does not actively request banner or rewarded video ads. We have not added separate advertising, analytics or social plugins. Before enabling ads or adding services that process personal information, we will update the relevant data, purpose and third-party disclosures and obtain consent where required by law. Ads or services independently provided by the host app are governed by that app’s own disclosures.

2.3 Hosting of this privacy page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may process access logs such as your IP address, browser or device information and request time, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page contains no embedded ads, analytics scripts or tracking pixels, and does not read your game saves.

3. Storage and retention

Levels, scores and settings are stored primarily in the host app’s local storage on your current device. We have not established a developer-operated cloud-save server for this edition. Clearing game or host-app data, uninstalling an app or changing devices may remove local progress, which we cannot restore from the cloud. Cache retention may differ between hosts.

Emails you send us are retained in the mailbox used for support and in necessary enquiry records. Unless the law requires otherwise, we retain them for the shortest period needed to answer your enquiry, investigate a problem, handle a rights request or resolve a related dispute, then delete or anonymise them.

Information independently processed by the platform, host app, email service or GitHub is subject to their own retention, location and international-transfer arrangements. Local game saves do not mean that all data handled by those services remains on your device or in one country. If we introduce processing that requires an international transfer of personal information, we will meet the applicable notice, consent and other legal requirements.

4. Use, disclosure and security

We use information only for the purposes and within the necessary scope explained here. We do not sell your personal information or use local game progress to create developer advertising profiles. Apart from the services described in this notice, your explicit request, lawfully obtained authorisation or legal requirements, we do not actively share, transfer or publicly disclose your personal information.

Before introducing additional sharing, transfers or public disclosures, we will provide the legally required information about recipients, purposes, methods and data categories, and obtain separate consent where required. Before using information beyond its original purpose or reasonable scope, we will notify you and follow the required consent process. We do not treat continued play as blanket consent to new processing.

We use access controls and other reasonable safeguards proportionate to the information we handle, limiting access to enquiry information to those who need it to resolve the matter. If an information security incident occurs, we will take remedial action and meet applicable notification and reporting duties.

5. Your rights and permission choices

You can contact 371652098@qq.com to request access to, a copy of, correction or deletion of personal information we actually handle, to withdraw consent, or to exercise other rights available under applicable law. We will verify only the information necessary to deal with your request and respond within the applicable legal time limit. If we cannot fulfil a request, we will explain why.

You may manage local saves through the game-data or cache controls provided by your host app, where available. The location of these controls and whether they can clear only this game’s data depend on the host. Clearing local saves may delete your levels, scores and skin records. We currently have no developer server that can remotely restore or selectively delete saves on your device.

Permissions granted to a host or platform can be adjusted through its settings, privacy or permission controls; names vary by platform. You can also stop using the game. Withdrawing consent does not affect lawful processing carried out before withdrawal, but features that rely on the relevant permission may stop working. For host or platform accounts and data processed independently by those services, contact the relevant provider.

6. Children’s information

Minors should read this notice with a parent or guardian and follow the host platform’s age and usage requirements. For children under 14, or other situations in which the law requires parental consent, we will obtain that consent and provide the protection required by applicable law. If a parent or guardian believes we have improperly handled a child’s information, they can contact the email address above. After checking the matter, we will delete the information, stop processing it or take other appropriate action as required. Please do not include a child’s identity details in support messages unless they are necessary to resolve the issue.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this notice when game features or information-handling practices change and show the revision date on this page. For material changes to processing purposes, data categories or your rights, we will provide notice before they take effect through an in-game message, platform announcement or another appropriate channel, and obtain renewed consent where required.
